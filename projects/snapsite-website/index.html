Everything a field crew needs, nothing they don't

GPS-tagged photo grouping

Every photo is stamped with its location and grouped into the right project the moment it's taken. No manual sorting back at the truck.

Interactive map view

See every active project as a pin on the map. Tap one to jump straight into its photos, notes, and status.

Workgroups with lead visibility

Inspectors capture their own sites while the lead account sees every inspector and every location in one consolidated view.

Flexible photo sharing

Share with the whole workgroup, hand-pick specific inspectors, or generate a public link. You decide who sees it.

Voice & text notes, plus AI reports

Complete & approval workflow
